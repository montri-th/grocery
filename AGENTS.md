# CityMETER Grocery — owner preferences

Set 2026-10-22 (release 0.1), carried over from CityMETER Luxury Brands 0.5 and adapted to an administrative hierarchy. Release 0.2 (owner, 22 Oct 2026, second round) adds the venue level and the two-point view in the same form as Luxury Brands, on top of the store–venue–building joins of commit 037ff18.

- Apply current LDS 0.9.7 (v0.9.7-owner.1, standalone-0.9.7-r1) and preserve the map-first CityMETER layout of Luxury Brands. Colours come only from `assets/theme.css` and `js/scales.js`; never approximate a value by eye.
- Area unit (owner choice 2026-10-22, “A+อปท”): the drill path is the production one — ประเทศไทย › จังหวัด › อำเภอ/เขต › อปท./ย่าน › สถานที่. Outside Bangkok the finest level is the local administration (เทศบาล/อบต., DOLA code); inside Bangkok it is the CityMETER Locale (ย่าน). Never invent an area, a boundary or an id; the production ids/codes are the keys (`province=BKK`, `district=1039`, `unit=<DOLA code | locale uuid>`).
- First Aha (owner choice 2026-10-22, “สาขากระจุกเทียบคน”): the first screen states one computed finding — “30% ของสาขาอยู่ใน 3 จังหวัดนี้ ซึ่งมีคนแค่ 13% ของประเทศ” — and the same question is recomputed at every level (country → provinces, province → districts, district → units) and for every filter. Show the share of stores, the share of residents, the named areas and the immediate actions (ดูทุก…, เปรียบเทียบ…) before complex filters. No onboarding gate. Snapshot numbers in the handoff JSON are fixtures, not UI constants.
- Counts are production counts: a store's district is its source subdistrict membership, its local unit the production overlay membership (15 stores sit in two overlapping units and count in both, as in production; the Aha share counts each store once). An area's count is the same everywhere: a unit shows all its production members also inside its district's comparison (25 units straddle a district border), while a district's shares — the Aha numerator and the ranking's store share — count only the district's own stores (`inArea`). Population is the registered population CityMETER uses; never estimate a missing population — show no data, never zero, and drop the population comparison when a compared area lacks it.
- Map colour: the area metric switches between จำนวนสาขา (LDS `count`) and ต่อ 1 หมื่นคน (LDS `density.capita`, denominator in the legend), exact LUT HEX on both themes. measured_zero (sample 0 + zero outline) ≠ no_data (neutral) ≠ out_of_scope (not drawn).
- Colour scales always use all 41 LDS samples (owner rule 2026-10-22, “Color scale ขอให้ใช้ 41 scales ตาม LDS เสมอ”) — map, legend, rankings and matrices; never a 3/5/7/9-class projection, never a CSS gradient or interpolated colour. `js/scales.js#buildScale` (`rank-41`): shown values (display-rounded) take their mid-rank among the compared values, lowest → sample 0, highest → sample 40, ties share a sample; the legend (`js/ramp-legend.js`) shows the whole 41-swatch ramp, marks the samples in use and ticks min · median · max. Rank, not linear: a linear ramp would put 70 of 77 provinces in the first five samples. Text on a sample takes the better foundation ink; where neither reaches 4.5:1 (count 27–29, density.capita 31–35) the number goes on a neutral backplate — never recolour a sample. The five-class tokens in `assets/theme.css` are single-colour cues (exact samples 0/10/20/30/40), not a scale.
- Focus + quiet context: draw detail only for the focused part — the top areas of the Aha (auto), explicit `?areas=`, the open unit/venue/store, or everything only when the user asks for ดูทุก… (`?areas=all`). Everything else stays visible but quiet: outline-only areas, quiet labels, grey context dots without logos and outside the tab order.
- The product statement is a winning strategy (Who / What / Which / How) — read `PRODUCT_STATEMENT.md` before changing what the first screen argues.
- Basemap and massing are context: colours only from the `--basemap-*` tokens; the style is generated by `js/map-style.js`; building heights come from OpenFreeMap/OSM and are never quoted as surveyed facts. Selected raw footprints use the existing map active-layer tokens and exclude overlapping context surfaces.
- Building links have two separate meanings: `point_inside` accepts one unique footprint with the source point at least 10 m inside its edge; an exact venue-name/source-id crosswalk may show named-venue reference geometry independently. Show the latter as “อาคารของสถานที่ตามชื่อสาขา”, never upgrade the point's spatial status from it. Boundary candidates, overlaps and missing footprints remain unresolved; never snap to a nearby building, move source coordinates or infer tenancy from either relation.
- Owner exception (25 Oct 2026): only supplemental PARC `bigc-official-15073` selects the explicitly confirmed Maps point `[100.6477432, 13.6671705]` through `selectedCoordinate`; raw official fields/sourceFacts remain immutable. Keep the Maps source/date label and boundary result under the unchanged 10 m guard; this authorizes no other coordinate move, snapping, strict-inside promotion, height or operating-status inference.
- Point-building source priority is native host hit → verified reference host hit → Microsoft gap fill. Retain earlier boundary/ambiguous outcomes; later-source interiors cannot promote them. Native no-hit fallback keeps native status/query coverage and a source-comparison reason. Never count alternative-provider overlaps or 3D parts as extra independent hosts. All 27,607 source rows are processed, but source coverage is not a complete census of physical buildings.
- Heights are source/model context, never surveyed facts. Microsoft -1/missing source height remains null; 3 m is display-only with unknown_display_fallback. Show short source/height facts and official dataset-page links, not raw bulk-download links. Preserve original geometry, snapshot IDs, provider attribution and CDLA-Permissive-2.0 / CC-BY-4.0 / ODbL roles in building-sources.json.
- Camera presets (`js/camera.js`) never move or scale data: `plan` for anything wider than 0.3°, on mobile portrait for areas and when 3D is off, `city` for a compact district overview, `model` for a local unit or a store outside every venue, and `eye` (มุมสถาปนิก, two-point: pitch 75 / FOV 24) at venue level only — on by default there (a venue, or a store whose branch name names a venue; `?cam=model` opts out; the toolbar toggle is disabled elsewhere and in 2D), never elsewhere. While the mobile sheet covers the map the venue view waits (plan in the strip above the sheet) and closing the sheet re-frames it in `eye`. `frameVenue` keeps every footprint and roof vertex (projected with its display height) and the venue's store points inside the padded viewport, zooming out at most one level beyond the footprint fit. Keep every 3D preset at pitch ≤ 90° − fov/2 − 3°: MapLibre 5.6.2 draws fill-extrusion displaced or not at all once the horizon enters the viewport.
- One dot always represents one source row; never aggregate or cluster stores, never move a coordinate. Every row is a WebGL dot (`js/stores-layer.js`, fading in at zoom 7–8 so the country reads as a choropleth first); from zoom 14 the rows in focus become HTML pins with brand logos (≤ 450 pins, ≤ 250 logos, `js/pins.js`), keyboard-navigable.
- Venue = a place a branch name or address names (sheet `07_VENUES`, name-based). This relation and its reference geometry do not confirm that a store occupies the venue; a point-in-building result describes the source coordinate only. The venue name stays the identity; a logo never changes a row; shared operator/department-store banners keep their role label; collecting logo files is not a trademark licence.
- Breadcrumb is ประเทศไทย › จังหวัด › อำเภอ/เขต › อปท./ย่าน › สถานที่; a store is the panel heading, never a crumb; a store whose branch name names a venue ends its trail at that venue; production links (`/v3/citymeter/{ABBR}[/M/{code}|/L/{uuid}]?d=grocery`) go out, never in.
- Venue level (owner 2026-10-22, release 0.2, “in the same form as Luxury Brands”): สถานที่ is a level of its own. At district/unit level the accepted reference buildings of the venues the focused rows name are drawn as “อาคารของสถานที่” (`js/venue-level.js#areaVenueBuildings`) and a click opens the venue; the unit panel lists “สถานที่ตามชื่อสาขา” first. At venue level only that venue's rows carry detail and only its buildings are blue — its reference footprints/parts, or without a reference the footprints its own points fall inside (“อาคารตามพิกัดสาขา”); never another venue's or a nearest building. The venue solid is drawn by `js/venue-xray.js` on top of the 3D pass (scissored depth clear) and hit-tests its own triangles, so it stays visible and clickable at any pan, pitch or zoom; in the `eye` scene the massing goes very quiet (`SCENE.eye`) and focus-tier buildings between the camera and the venue become see-through. Draw order is depth order: buildings-as-data before the first label layer, massing tiers inserted before them, the x-ray last — never reorder casually. Click priority: store dots → drawn buildings → x-ray solid → areas (`originalEvent.__citymeterHandled`). A drawn building is evidence about a name or a plotted point, never tenancy, floor, height or status.
- Keep generic caveats and research workflow residue out of ordinary UI, popups, details, tooltips and help. Provenance and limits live in the store facts, `DATA_METHOD.md` and the handoff. Never replace uncertainty with fabricated facts; never show missing as zero; status is “อยู่ในรายการสาขาของแบรนด์” — no store was re-checked one by one.
- Copy: short, natural Thai — สาขา (one source row) / แบรนด์ / สถานที่ / อปท. / ย่าน / ประชากร / ต่อ 1 หมื่นคน; an abbreviated noun takes a space (“ดูทุก อปท.”, `js/model.js#withNoun`).
- Use approved LDS Rounded outline navigation glyphs (nav-7, FILL 0, weight 300). Pictograms in metric tiles and headings come from the 9-glyph subset (`PICTOGRAM_CONTRACT`, owner test exception); the text label stays the meaning.
- Charts: matrix cells are equal in size; only the number and its sample carry meaning. The population bar is neutral, never a data colour. No CSV download control. No decorative brackets or coloured left rails. Review Thai at desktop and 390 px in light and dark before claiming a change is done; local renders are not proof of a live release — run `npm run verify:live`.
